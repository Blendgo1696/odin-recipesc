Burger Recipe

A classic homemade burger recipe features a seasoned beef patty, fresh vegetables, and a creamy sauce on a toasted bun.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 bun
  • lettuce
  • tomato
  • cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
  2. Form the ground beef into patties and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Grill the patties for 3-4 minutes on each side, or until cooked to your liking.
  4. Toast the buns on the grill for a minute or two.
  5. Assemble the burgers by placing the patties on the bottom buns, followed by lettuce, tomato, and cheese.
  6. Top with the top buns and serve.
