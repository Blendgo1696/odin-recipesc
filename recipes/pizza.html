Pizza Recipe

A classic homemade pizza recipe features a crispy crust, a tangy tomato sauce, and a variety of toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pizza dough
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup parmesan cheese
  • 2 cups marinara sauce

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Transfer the dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  4. Spread the marinara sauce over the dough, leaving a border for the crust.
  5. Top with mozzarella and parmesan cheese.
  6. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown.
