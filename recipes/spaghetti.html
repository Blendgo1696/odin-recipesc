Spaghetti Recipe

A classic homemade spaghetti recipe features al dente pasta, a rich meat tomato sauce, and a creamy cheese blend

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spaghetti noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 1 cup parmesan cheese
  • 2 cups marinara sauce

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the spaghetti noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain the excess fat.
  4. Add the marinara sauce and simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. In a mixing bowl, combine the ricotta cheese and parmesan cheese.
  6. Assemble the lasagna by layering the noodles, meat sauce, and cheese mixture.
  7. Bake for 30 minutes or until the top is golden brown.
